# Useful resources to go through before starting

Here is a list of resources that students doing their thesis can read to get the maximum potential out of their projects.
Generally, these can be used not only by students doing their thesis, but more broadly to poeple who are starting getting involved in research.
Part of these resources have been found thanks to BlueDot Impact's courses.

- [How to have a good research meeting with your advisor](https://medium.com/@jurgens_24580/reflections-on-strategies-for-successful-meetings-with-undergraduate-researchers-ae22306ecd8d) 
- [How to set up slides for keeping track of your work & present](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/i3b9uQfjJjJkwZF4f/tips-on-empirical-research-slides)
